Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

Cheremo NyumbaLink ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website located at nyumbalink.com. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal data when you interact with our platform.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Credentials: Account registration details including name, username, email, phone number, and password hashes.
Verification Logs: For Landlords and Property Agents, we collect government-issued ID copies, title deed scans, and utility records to verify listing legitimacy.
Activity Logs: Search history, property bookings, contact queries, chat messages, and favorites collection.

2. How We Use Your Information

To provide, manage, and optimize the house hunting and listing platforms. To authenticate users, prevent malicious bookings, reduce identity scams, and process landlord applications.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal details. Tenant details are shared with landlords only when a booking is confirmed, a chat is initiated, or a rental application is explicitly submitted.
