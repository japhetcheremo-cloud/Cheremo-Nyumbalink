Frequently Asked Questions

Get instant solutions to common issues regarding renting, listings, and landlord verification processes.

Renting Questions

How do I book a viewing on the platform?

To book a viewing, log in to your tenant account, navigate to the property listing you are interested in, select your preferred date and time on the booking widget, write a short introductory note, and submit. The landlord or property agent will receive the booking request instantly and accept/decline.

Are there viewing fees on Cheremo NyumbaLink?

No. A core value of Cheremo NyumbaLink is the total elimination of viewing fees. Booking a house viewing on this platform is completely free. We strongly warn tenants against paying any external "viewing fees" or "commission fees" before verifying properties physically.

Landlord & Listing Questions

How long does it take for a property listing to appear on search result pages?

To maintain high quality and prevent rental scams, all new property listings enter a "Pending" status and are reviewed manually by our administrators. This validation process usually takes between 2 to 12 hours. Once approved, the listing goes live instantly, and the landlord receives a notification.

What is the "Verified Landlord" badge?

The verification badge is given to landlords or agents who submit physical proof of identity and property ownership documents (e.g. title deeds, utility bills, business certificates). Verified listings are prioritized in searches and command significantly higher trust levels from tenants.

Security & Fraud Protection

What should I do if I encounter a suspicious listing or landlord?

If a listing has false coordinates, incorrect images, or the landlord requests booking fees before physical meetings, click the "Report" flag on the property details page immediately. Our compliance department will investigate the issue and suspend fraudulent accounts within 4 hours.
