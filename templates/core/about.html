Who We Are

Connecting Tenants & Landlords Directly

Cheremo NyumbaLink was founded with the singular vision of eliminating rental fraud, reducing search fatigue, and introducing transparent communication platforms.

We believe that finding a house shouldn't require paying ridiculous viewing fees or navigating scam groups. By enforcing strict landlord verification, providing video tours, and facilitating real-time chat, we restore trust to the house hunting process.

Our Mission

To make rental hunting stress-free by building digital channels that promote direct landlord relations, transparent listings, and automated booking schedules.

Our Vision

Core Values
